Circuit de la Vallée

A fast, flowing countryside circuit built partly on closed public roads: a long tree-lined straight broken by two chicanes, a flat-out kink, a slow right-hander at the end of the straight, sweeping esses through the forest and a tight final chicane before the pit straight.

Endurance tips: tyres are fitted cold (no tyre warmers), so take it easy for the first lap of a stint. Prototypes have no ABS, so modulate the brake in heavy braking zones. Hypercars deploy hybrid power above 190 km/h and regenerate under braking. Faster classes will lap you, so hold your line when the blue flag is shown. In the rain, switch to wet tyres at the next stop.
